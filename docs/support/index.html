Support

Find quick answers below or open a GitHub issue if something is not working as expected.

Where is my history stored?

Your push-up history is stored locally on your device. Push Daily does not require an account and does not upload your history to a Push Daily server.

Does Push Daily sync between devices?

No. Version 1.0 is intentionally local-first and does not include account or cross-device sync features.

How do I correct a previous day?

Open Progress, choose the date in the calendar, then use the minus or plus button to adjust that day's count.

What should I include in a report?

Include the app version, device model, operating-system version, steps that reproduce the problem, and what you expected to happen. Screenshots are helpful, but remove any personal information before posting.
