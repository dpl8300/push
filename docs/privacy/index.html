Push Daily

Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026

Summary

Push Daily is designed to work without an account. Your push-up history is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to Push Daily or its developer.

Data stored by the app

The app stores the dates and push-up counts you enter so it can display totals, streaks, charts, and calendar history. This information remains in the app's local storage on your device.

Data collection

Push Daily does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your push-up history or personal information. The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or third-party crash-reporting services.

Accounts and deletion

Push Daily does not create user accounts. You can remove the locally stored history by deleting the app. Your operating system may include app data in device backups that you control through your device or cloud-backup settings.

Support and external websites

When you choose Privacy Policy or Support inside the app, your browser opens a website hosted by GitHub. If you submit a support issue, the information you provide is processed by GitHub under its own privacy terms. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public support issue.

Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be posted here with a revised effective date. For questions, use the Push Daily support page.
